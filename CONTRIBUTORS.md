# Contributors and acknowledgments

Armbian for Xiaomi Mi 5 is the result of sustained development, repeated
real-device testing, and contributions from the wider Linux ecosystem.

## Maintainer

**[AnggaWekape](https://github.com/anggawahyudikp)** leads the project, provides
the Mi 5 hardware, performs physical validation, defines the server requirements,
and makes release decisions.

## AI development assistants

The project acknowledges **Google Gemini** and **OpenAI ChatGPT** for their role
as AI development assistants during the development process. These credits
recognize the tools; human maintainers remain responsible for reviewing changes,
validating hardware behavior, and deciding what is released.

## Upstream communities

Thanks to the contributors to Linux, Armbian, Debian, the Android Open Source
Project, and the Xiaomi Mi 5/MSM8996 device-support community. Their kernel,
userspace, build tooling, documentation, and hardware research provide the
foundation for this project.
